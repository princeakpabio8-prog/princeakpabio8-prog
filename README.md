# Selected Projects

## HealLink

**AI-Powered Digital Health Platform for Trusted Healthcare**

HealLink connects patients with verified healthcare professionals while helping communities verify medical information through AI-assisted verification.

The platform combines telehealth, practitioner verification, appointment management, and intelligent health claim verification to improve access to trusted healthcare.

## VeriEdit AI

**Trust Infrastructure for Artificial Intelligence**

VeriEdit AI helps organizations verify, audit, and structure AI-generated outputs before they become business decisions.

The platform focuses on responsible AI, governance, transparency, and evidence-based verification across enterprise workflows.

**Repository**

https://github.com/princeakpabio8-prog/VeriEditAI

## VeriLex

**AI-Assisted Legal Preparation Platform**

Built on top of VeriEdit AI, VeriLex helps individuals prepare legal matters before speaking with legal professionals.

The platform assists with contract analysis, evidence organization, AI-assisted document review, and structured case preparation while supporting—not replacing—professional legal advice.

# Engineering Philosophy

Technology should solve meaningful problems.

I believe the strongest software products are built by understanding people first and then designing systems that make difficult decisions easier.

My engineering principles are simple:

- Solve meaningful problems.
- Build trustworthy systems.
- Design for clarity.
- Ship early and improve continuously.
- Treat reliability as a product feature.
- Keep humans responsible for important decisions.
